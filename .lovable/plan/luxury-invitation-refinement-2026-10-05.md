# Luxury Invitation Refinement

## What will change
- Keep the existing single-page invitation, couple photo, countdown, event details, RSVP flow, and scrolling experience.
- Replace the envelope with a full-screen ivory opening featuring “A & M”, “AZIZ & MALAK”, a language switcher, and a cinematic open transition.
- Add one-language-at-a-time English and Arabic modes; Arabic will switch the complete invitation and RSVP form to RTL.
- Refine the page into restrained editorial stationery using warm ivory, muted champagne, dusty rose, warm brown, thin dividers, generous whitespace, and quieter motion.
- Restyle the hero and countdown as a minimal editorial composition rather than boxed website cards.
- Add an in-page “Special Moments” composition with two compact photo positions, prepared for the two photos when uploaded.
- Update the in-page venue to Kerdasa, Giza and connect “View Location” to the exact supplied Google Maps link.
- Add a small persistent Music On/Off control. Audio will only start from the Open Invitation click and continue during scrolling.

## Music handling
- “I Found You” is not specific enough to verify as royalty-free, and no licensed audio file was uploaded. I will not embed an unknown commercial recording.
- The invitation will include a ready audio slot and graceful unavailable state. A confirmed royalty-free file can be added directly later without changing the design.

- Pass the selected language into the countdown and RSVP dialog.
- Preserve local RSVP saving and existing reveal behavior while reducing decorative effects.
- Make mobile the primary layout, prevent horizontal overflow, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Verify the opening, language switching, countdown, location link, RSVP flow, and mobile/desktop presentation in the running preview.
